# Own the court lines, close before redirecting

The best athletic, racket-sport and venue sites examined share one skeleton: **one static full-bleed hero, a 2–5 word headline, one primary CTA, a neutral UI, and colour taken from the photography**. What makes each one distinctive is **typography and one owned graphic device, not motion**. Venue sites (Barry's, Equinox, IMG) add a low-commitment first step ("Book Your First Class", "Visit a Club") and a practical location card. India's flagship badminton academies do neither. Their sites lead with legacy, show no fees and have no enquiry path ([PGBA](https://gopichandacademy.com/), [PPBA](https://www.ppba.in/)). That matters for Celestial's Badminton Academy (CBA) because the Hudle and Playo pages it redirects to show it at **3.92–4.0 (26 ratings) next to competitors rated 4.21–4.31** ([Playo Noida](https://playo.co/venues/noida/sports/badminton)). CBA's own site therefore has to do the persuading before the hand-off. No examined site uses court geometry or shuttle flight as a design language; BWF's shuttle-skirt-like ring logo is the only near miss. That leaves CBA a motif to own, and shuttle physics (pure drag, fast deceleration, a "triangular" path) gives it a signature animation that is cheap to build. The UX evidence is strongest for clarity: a static hero, visible navigation, short forms, real local trust signals, WCAG 2.2 and Core Web Vitals. It is weak for persuasion devices such as sticky CTAs. None of the sites published conversion data.

## Seven principles that survive the move to badminton

**1. One viewport, one job, key facts visible.** Nike and Wimbledon give the hero a single message and a single CTA. Eyetracking shows **~57% of viewing time falls above the fold and 74% within two screenfuls** ([NN/g](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/scrolling-and-attention/)). CBA's first screen should show what it is, where it is, the hours, "from ₹450", and a "Book a Court" button. It should not auto-rotate: in a 5-slide carousel each offer was visible only **20% of the time** ([NN/g](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/auto-forwarding/)).

**2. Two doors, each with a ladder.** CBA sells two things, so the page should split them early: "Play tonight" (pay-and-play) and "Train seriously" (academy). Each door gets a low-commitment first step, the way Barry's splits first-timers from returning clients and IMG runs consultation before application. For CBA that means a WhatsApp free trial, which already exists in its Instagram bio ([Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/celestialsbadmintonacademy/?hl=en)).

**3. Group the choices and offer a default.** Choice overload averages "virtually zero" across 50 experiments ([Scheibehenne et al. 2010](https://ideas.repec.org/a/oup/jconrs/v37y2010i3p409-425.html)). It does appear when options are complex and preferences uncertain ([Chernev et al. 2015](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S1057740814000916)), which describes a first-time parent choosing a batch well. Group programmes by age and level, use a Yonex-style picker, and offer "Not sure? Book an assessment" as the default.

**4. Prove the place is real and current.** Stanford's guidelines start with a physical address and photos of the premises ([Stanford](https://credibility.stanford.edu/guidelines/)). NN/g adds upfront pricing, current content and links to third-party reviews ([NN/g](https://nngroup.com/articles/trustworthy-design/)). ASICS shows how to do it with named people; PPBA's news from 2014 shows the opposite.

**5. Distinctiveness comes from one owned motif plus type, not effects.** adidas's label boxes, Roland-Garros's clay colour and Nike's condensed caps are each recognisable on their own. CBA's equivalent is court-mat green, court-line white and a condensed display face.

**6. Motion has to earn its place.** UI motion should take **100–500 ms** ([NN/g](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/animation-duration/)), respect `prefers-reduced-motion` ([WCAG 2.3.3](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/animation-from-interactions.html)), and never block the largest paint (LCP ≤ 2.5 s at p75 on mobile, per [web.dev](https://web.dev/articles/defining-core-web-vitals-thresholds)). Not even On, the most motion-led site examined, showed visible reduced-motion handling.

**7. Close the gap before the redirect.** Rocket Padel shows that a third-party hand-off can work. Because Playo then lists CBA among **179 Noida venues** with ratings beside it, the trust-building has to happen on CBA's site, and every outbound button should say where it goes.

### Four recurring patterns worth adopting

A **dual-path sticky header** with "Book a Court · ₹450+" (which opens a Hudle/Playo chooser) and "Free Trial" (WhatsApp). On mobile, it should hide on scroll down and reappear on scroll up ([NN/g](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/sticky-headers/)), and disappear while a form field has focus ([Smashing](https://www.smashingmagazine.com/2023/05/sticky-menus-ux-guidelines/)). A **Barry's-style location card** with the address, metro distance, hours, parking, the non-marking-shoes rule and a map that loads on tap. **Parallel route cards** in the Wimbledon style, one per way in. A **condensed display face paired with a neutral text face**, with a serif eyebrow label for a premium cue.

### Three conventions worth challenging

**The legacy-first hero.** PGBA and PPBA lead with trophies, and On leads with mood. A local venue should lead with the visitor's decision and move credentials to the second row. **Hidden practical information.** Commerce brands, Barry's and Rocket push hours and prices off the homepage, but CBA's transparent ₹450 is an advantage it should show. **Locking zoom.** Four of the five big racket sites plus PGBA set `user-scalable=no`, an accessibility failure CBA should simply not repeat.

### Three badminton-specific opportunities

**Court geometry as the layout system.** None of the examined sites draws court lines. The 13.4 × 6.1 m court proportion can set the hero aspect ratio, and service lines can serve as section dividers. **A physically accurate shuttle as the signature motion.** A hero smash that slows sharply, flips cork-first and drops almost vertically onto the "Book" button would be recognisable to any player, and nobody else is doing it. **"Courts today" as live state.** BWF's "LIVE" strip, adapted to "4 of 7 courts free tonight" and combined with the programme picker, would make the site useful as well as attractive, but only if the availability data is real.

## A shuttle decelerates, flips and drops, so animate exactly that

A shuttle is a **~5 g pure-drag projectile**. Its terminal velocity is **U∞ ≈ 6.2–6.7 m/s**, its drag coefficient is **C_D ≈ 0.65**, and its aerodynamic length is **L = U∞²/g ≈ 4.3–4.6 m** ([Cohen et al. 2015](https://rainbow.ldeo.columbia.edu/~alexeyk/Papers/Cohen_2015_New_J._Phys._17_063001.pdf); [Collet 2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.01412)). At smash speeds drag is ~400× the shuttle's weight, so speed decays exponentially with distance, **v = V₀·e^(−r/L)**. That means speed halves roughly every **L·ln2 ≈ 3.0–3.2 m**. Time of flight is **t = (L/V₀)(e^(r/L) − 1)**, so a 300 km/h smash covers 10 m in ~440 ms ([Collet 2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.01412)). The path is **"triangular"**: a near-straight launch, a tight apex and an almost vertical fall. Range saturates at about L regardless of power (13.8 m maximum measured) ([Cohen et al. 2015](https://rainbow.ldeo.columbia.edu/~alexeyk/Papers/Cohen_2015_New_J._Phys._17_063001.pdf)). A measured smash deviated only ~10 cm from a straight line over 9.5 m ([Collet 2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.01412)). This is why a parabolic CSS ease looks wrong to players.

The shuttle **flips cork-first** because its centre of mass sits ~3 cm ahead of its centre of pressure. The flip takes **~5 ms at 50 m/s and 9–39 ms at 10–27 m/s**, and the wobble settles within ~1 m ([Collet 2023](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.11155); [Cohen et al. 2015](https://rainbow.ldeo.columbia.edu/~alexeyk/Papers/Cohen_2015_New_J._Phys._17_063001.pdf)). The feather overlap makes the shuttle **spin anticlockwise** at ~19–84 rps depending on speed ([Hart & Potts 2020](https://www.mdpi.com/2504-3900/49/1/28)).

| Animation parameter | Value to use |
|---|---|
| Equation (per frame) | `a = g − (g/U∞²)·|v|·v` (vector); U∞ = 6.5 m/s |
| Launch ÷ terminal speed | Smash 15–20 · Clear 6–8 · Drop/net 1.5–3 |
| Screen scale | Map 13.4 m court length to the viewport; keep L ≈ ⅓ of the court |
| Flip | Start cork-back or tilted; rotate to cork-first over 150–250 ms at 10–20× slow motion |
| Wobble | Damped ±10–20°, settling within ~1 m of travel (amplitude is an estimate, not measured) |
| Spin | 15–40 rps aliases at 60 fps; show slowed rotation or blurred feathers |
| Total duration | Real smash ~0.4–0.7 s; a decorative flight under ~1.5 s is inferred from motion guidance |

In practice, run a fixed-step Euler integration of that one equation on canvas or SVG after a static first paint, using transforms only. Under `prefers-reduced-motion`, show a static frame of the flight path instead. Slow-motion playback must scale every time constant by the same factor, or the deceleration stops looking real.

## What this research could not see

**Motion was largely unobservable.** The screenshots were static and the club pages were text-only scrapes, so transitions, scroll effects, sticky-bar behaviour and GSAP effects were inferred from markup, not watched. adidas and ASICS blocked direct fetches (403). **No conversion or A/B data exists** for any examined design; every "why it works" claim is interpretation. **No performance audit** (LCP/CLS) was run. Only **homepages** were examined, and these are campaign-rotated, so the heroes will change. The academy baseline rests on **two Indian academies** (PPBA later failed DNS). Wilson, HEAD, UK/European clubs, Equinox club pages (404) and Game4Padel were not captured. **CBA facts need client confirmation**: court surface (PVC vs wooden), court count, coach roster, batch timings, coaching fees and the source of the 279-review figure. On physics, **wobble amplitude is unmeasured** and the sources disagree on whether spin raises or lowers drag. The WCAG 2.2 AA wording was cited from the Recommendation, not re-fetched.

## Conclusion

The real competitive gap is the persuasion layer, not visual polish. CBA's booking platforms already hold the facts, and they show those facts beside better-rated rivals. The site's job is to arrive at that comparison having already won: transparent price, real courts, named coaches, a low-risk trial. It should be visually ownable through court geometry rather than borrowed sportswear-campaign gloss.

Accurate shuttle physics also costs almost nothing in performance: one drag equation, under a second of motion, a static fallback. That makes it a rare signature that is both distinctive and evidence-compliant. The first build should treat the sticky bar, the WhatsApp CTA and the "Courts today" strip as experiments to measure, not settled best practice, because the research behind them is weakest.
